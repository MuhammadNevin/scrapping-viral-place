The victim reported the incident to the Propam division, while the West Sumatra Regional Police are currently investigating the event that has gone viral on social media. A video clip of the incident was uploaded by the Facebook account Susi Suzanna, the victim's mother. The video was then shared by several Instagram accounts and went viral, including @sumbarkareh_id. The original clip of the video shows a police car with license plate number 9-III suddenly cutting in and stopping the video recording car. A man suspected to be the Director of Criminal Investigation at the West Sumatra Regional Police, Senior Commissioner Teddy Fanani, got out and chased the victim. The alleged perpetrator was heard scolding the victim, who repeatedly apologized. "Are members of the West Sumatra Regional Police's Criminal Investigation Directorate taking the law into their own hands?" Susi Suzanna wrote in the caption of the video on her Facebook account. Bill Irzano (23), the victim of the assault, stated that the incident occurred on Friday afternoon while he was on his way to Minangkabau International Airport, Padang Pariaman. At that time, Bill, who was driving a car, wanted to overtake the police service vehicle. He honked the horn and activated the left turn signal before overtaking. After his car moved ahead, the driver of the police service vehicle also intended to overtake. "When I was about to overtake, the driver was shocked," said Bill, Friday afternoon. After the incident, the police service vehicle stopped the victim's car, and four individuals immediately approached Bill. The victim took the initiative to apologize, while the alleged perpetrator accused the victim's actions of endangering his life. "Actually, I was just following the route while driving my car, not taking his path either. Suddenly, he became arrogant and hit me. I was punched," said Bill. As a result of the incident, Bill's eyes were red and swollen. His glasses were also broken from the beating. "I want to have a medical examination and report it to the West Sumatra Regional Police," he said. Separately, the Head of the Public Relations Division of the West Sumatra Regional Police, Kombes Susmelawati Rosya, confirmed that the individuals involved in the altercation in the viral video are personnel of the West Sumatra Regional Police.
